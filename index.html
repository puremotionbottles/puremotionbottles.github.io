Questions

Good to know.

How does the self-cleaning function work?

Press the cap sensor to activate a 60-second UV-C cycle. The bottle can also run automatic cleaning cycles periodically to help keep the interior fresher between manual washes.

Can I put the bottle in the dishwasher?

No. Hand wash the bottle body and wipe the lid clean. Because the cap contains electronics, it should not be submerged.

How long does the battery last?

With typical use, a full charge can last up to one week. Charging takes around two hours via USB-C.

Does it keep drinks hot and cold?

Yes. Double-wall stainless-steel insulation keeps drinks hot for up to 12 hours and cold for up to 24 hours.

What colors are available?

PureMotion is offered in Canyon Sand, Graphite Black and Slate Green.
